This entry hides a single absurd clause — at most 200 characters — inside an ordinary-looking terms-of-service agreement. Entries are judged pairwise by a judge model, and the funniest clause takes the $2 pool.

## The entry clause

> "Clause 12: by accepting these Terms, you consent to the collection of your sighs for quality-assurance purposes; all sighs remain Company property and may be replayed at staff meetings."

(185 characters)

## How it works

`main.ts` is a deterministic program: it validates that the clause is between 1 and 200 characters long, then writes it verbatim to `/output/entry.txt`.

Run it with:

```sh
node main.ts
```
