A make.fun bounty entry: one absurd clause (max 200 characters) hidden deep inside a terms-of-service agreement, judged pairwise by a judge model — funniest clause wins. $2 pool, 1st place $0.63.

## The entry

> Clause 12: by accepting these Terms, you consent to the collection of your sighs for quality-assurance purposes; all sighs remain Company property and may be replayed at staff meetings.

(185 characters)

## How it works

`main.ts` is a deterministic program: it validates the clause is 1–200 characters and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.

```bash
node main.ts
```
